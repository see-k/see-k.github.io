"Autopilot" gets used loosely, but most systems marketed that way are really just prompt wrappers or scheduled workflows. Real autopilot means a system that can persist state, react to events, coordinate execution, and stay interruptible.

The hard parts are not just model quality. They are memory, routing, authority, safety boundaries, and the ability to decide what should happen next without losing control of the system.

A credible autopilot stack needs:

- event detection and prioritization
- persistent workflow state
- clear execution boundaries
- human approval classes
- loop resistance and auditability
